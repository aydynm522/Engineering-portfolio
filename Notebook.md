#plastic shredder design project 

Build a cheap and simple machine that shreds plastic into small pieces so it can be recycld.

*Main Parts
-Motor to power the machine
-Motor reducer
-Cutting shaft to shred the plastic
-Frame to hold everything together
-Cover to protect the moving parts

*Motor info
-Horsepower: 1.5 hp
-Speed: 3,450 rpm
-Voltage: 230–240 v
-Motor reducer: still Need to find info about 

*Measurements
-Motor length: 13.065 inches
-Motor height: 8.375 inches
-Motor width: 6.625 inches
-Shaft diameter: 0.375 inches
-Motor reducer length: About 7 inches
-Motor reducer width: About 6.5 inches

*Design Ideas
-Decide if we should use one shaft or two
-Make a strong frame for the motor
-Figure out how to connect the motor to the cutting shaft
-Add a cover to protect 
-Make the machine easy to clean

Steps for converting plastic (Before spliting into our shredder group)
Step 1 Collect the plastic.
Step 2 Sort it by type.
Step 3 Wash and dry it.
Step 4 Shred it into small pieces.
Step 5 Melt and mold the plastic if it is safe and suitable.
Step 6 Store or package the finished material.
